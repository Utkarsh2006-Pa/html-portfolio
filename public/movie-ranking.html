The Best Movies According to Utkarsh

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Dhurandhar

A high-intensity action thriller centered around covert operations, patriotism, and national security.

Uri The Surgical Strike

A military action film depicting the planning and execution of India’s surgical strikes after the Uri attack.

Mirzapur

A gritty crime saga about power, revenge, violence, and the struggle for control in the world of crime and politics.
